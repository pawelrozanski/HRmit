# 0006. MIT license

Date: 2026-10-19
Status: accepted

## Context

HRmit is a portfolio project, not a business. The goal is that recruiters, HR tech people and developers can read, run and reuse it with no friction.

## Decision

MIT.

## Consequences

- Anyone can use, modify and embed the code, including in closed-source products, with attribution.
- AGPL would have prevented a hosted SaaS fork from closing its source, but there is no business to protect and AGPL makes some companies refuse to even open the repository, which works against the portfolio goal.
- Dependencies must be MIT/Apache/BSD compatible; this is checked once in the roadmap's final review.
